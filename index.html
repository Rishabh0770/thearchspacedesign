Who We Are

ArchSpace Design

ArchSpace Design is a research-oriented structural and dynamic design firm, rooted in Solan, Himachal Pradesh. We are committed to building strong structures and achieving our goals through our expertise in designing and engineering.

Devoted to building dreams and creating spaces — with proficiency in project planning, designing, 2D drawings and 3D modelling, we make designs physically feasible through research and structural analysis. We monitor every project daily to assure quality and ensure construction is executed as per the plan.

ArchSpace Design believes that building designs are not just sturdy structures — it is about building dreams through every space, wall, and pathway to create new memories.
